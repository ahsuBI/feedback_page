Luma Cosmetics feedback chatbot

A conversational agent that collects customer feedback and rewards participation with a gamified spin wheel. Built with IBM Watson Assistant; I designed the conversation flows and deployed the front end.

Try it

  1. Open the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of this page.
  2. Tell Luma about a recent purchase — good or bad.
  3. Finish the feedback flow to unlock the spin wheel and win a reward.

The assistant may take a few seconds to load on first visit.

How it works

The bot guides customers through a short feedback conversation: it identifies the product, captures sentiment and specifics, and routes complaints differently from praise. Completing the flow triggers the spin wheel, turning a chore (giving feedback) into a small game — which is the point: response rates rise when there's something in it for the customer.
